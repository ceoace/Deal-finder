How it decides

For every address, it looks up what the house could sell for after it's fixed up (called the ARV), guesses how much fixing it up would cost, and figures out the most you could pay and still make money (called the MAO, or Maximum Allowable Offer). If the asking price is at or under the MAO, it's a deal. 👍
