Probate leads (the honest truth)

No Georgia county publishes a free, downloadable probate lead list. Probate filings live in each county's Probate Court, and most only offer a case-by-case docket search — no bulk export. Two realistic paths:

  • Paid list service (e.g. PropStream, BatchLeads, USLeadList) — these already aggregate probate + tax delinquent + pre-foreclosure leads by radius/zip and export clean CSVs. This is what most active wholesalers actually use, because scraping two dozen county court sites by hand doesn't scale.
  • Manual pull — search each county's Probate Court docket site for new filings, one at a time. Free, but slow.

Either way, once you have a list with an address column, this app treats it the same as a tax delinquent list — just import it.

About the 65-mile radius from 30313

A 65-mile radius from downtown Atlanta covers most of metro Atlanta plus the outer suburbs — roughly the counties listed above. Since county tax offices publish by county (not by mile radius), the practical way to work the radius is: pick the counties inside it, pull each county's list, and let this app do the deal math on whatever comes back.
